▸ ASL fingerspelling reference

A quick alphabet chart, drawn with the same hand model above. Fingerspelling is only one small corner of American Sign Language — full ASL has its own grammar and word order, distinct from spoken English, and isn't captured by spelling words out.

Handspeak runs entirely in your browser: speech recognition uses the Web Speech API (Chrome, Edge, Safari), and the hand is a hand-built, parametric SVG — no images, no video, no external hand-tracking data. The handshapes are a simplified, stylized approximation for demo and learning purposes, not a certified ASL reference.
